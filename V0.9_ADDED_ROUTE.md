## 2. Root problems to solve

### P1 — The decoded state is not constrained to the physical manifold

Let

\[
\mathcal M_{\rm phys}
=\{U:\nabla\cdot u=0,\;u|_{\partial\Omega}=u_{\rm BC}\}.
\]

The current route computes

\[
z_{t+1}=e^{A_t\Delta t}z_t,
\qquad
\hat U_{t+1}=D(z_{t+1}),
\]

but does not guarantee

\[
D(z_{t+1})\in\mathcal M_{\rm phys}.
\]

Soft observable penalties therefore permit improvements in velocity or vorticity to be purchased
by increased divergence.

### P2 — Observable objectives have unstable scales and conflicting gradients

The force loss can become ill-conditioned when the reference force amplitude is close to zero.
The latest run has a median validation physical loss below one but a maximum above forty. A fixed
weighted sum also permits negative gradient interaction between forecast, vorticity, divergence,
boundary and force objectives.

### P3 — Static condition dependence and genuine temporal adaptation are confounded

The current operator

\[
A_t=A_0+U\operatorname{diag}(\eta(c_t))V^\top
\]

allows the same context head to encode operating condition, current state and history. A high
operator-explained fraction does not prove that a time-varying operator is needed. The
latent-inferred dynamic-over-static result shows that a mean/static correction currently explains
almost as much as the dynamic branch.

### P4 — Training and acceptance horizons are misaligned

Decoded observables are trained at H4/H8/H16, while the decisive physical gate is evaluated at
H80. Latent long-rollout stability does not imply long-horizon physical consistency.

### P5 — A frozen representation may set the physical accuracy floor

If `D(E(U))` already violates incompressibility or boundary constraints, an operator-only update
cannot remove that floor. Increasing adaptive rank or epoch count is not a principled solution.

### P6 — The current data may not identify history dependence

A monotone `80 -> 120` smooth or abrupt condition change may be predictable from the present state
or instantaneous condition alone. A dynamic context cannot be scientifically identified unless
the data contain matched present states/conditions whose futures differ because their histories
differ.

# Phase 1 — Constrained Observable Optimization and Diagnostic Audit

## 4. Phase-1 scientific question

\[
\boxed{
\text{Can the frozen adaptive-operator route improve observables without violating physical
non-inferiority?}
}
\]

Phase 1 keeps the encoder, decoder, context encoder and `A0` frozen. It changes diagnostics and
optimization only.

## 5. Phase-1 diagnostic decomposition

Before retraining, evaluate four levels on the same locked split:

\[
U,
\quad D(E(U)),
\quad D(e^{A_0\Delta t}E(U)),
\quad D(e^{A_t\Delta t}E(U)).
\]

For every observable, report:

- numerical-data floor;
- autoencoder reconstruction floor;
- nominal dynamics contribution;
- adaptive dynamics contribution.

This audit determines whether a failed constraint is representation-owned or operator-owned.

Add a gradient interaction matrix over the trainable adaptive-operator parameters:

\[
G_{ij}
=
\frac{
\langle\nabla_\omega\mathcal L_i,\nabla_\omega\mathcal L_j\rangle
}{
\|\nabla_\omega\mathcal L_i\|\,\|\nabla_\omega\mathcal L_j\|+\epsilon
}.
\]

At minimum include forecast, each rollout horizon, velocity, vorticity, divergence, boundary,
lift and drag. Gradient balancing must not be enabled before this audit shows a scale or direction
conflict.

## 6. Robust observable normalization

For every observable `k`, compute a fixed train-only scale such as

\[
s_k=\operatorname{MAD}_{\rm train}(O_k)
\quad\text{or}\quad
s_k=\operatorname{RMS}_{\rm train}(O_k-\bar O_k).
\]

Use

\[
\mathcal L_k
=
\mathbb E\left[
\operatorname{Huber}\left(
\frac{\hat O_k-O_k}{s_k+\epsilon}
\right)
\right].
\]

Do not normalize lift or drag by the energy of the current sample or mini-batch. Persist all scales,
split fingerprints and provenance in the checkpoint and compact report.

Force observables should use a causal window objective:

\[
\mathcal L_F
=\mathcal L_{\rm waveform}
+\lambda_{\rm corr}(1-\operatorname{corr}(\hat F,F))
+\lambda_{\rm spec}\mathcal L_{\rm spectrum}.
\]

Hard peak selection remains evaluation-only; a differentiable spectral density or soft spectral
moment may be used for training.

## 7. Physics as constrained optimization

The primary forecast objective is minimized subject to non-inferiority:

\[
\min_\omega\mathcal L_{\rm forecast}+\mathcal L_{\rm rollout}+\mathcal L_{\rm force},
\]

\[
g_{\rm div}
=\mathcal L_{\rm div}^{A}-(1+\delta_{\rm div})\mathcal L_{\rm div}^{0}\le0,
\]

\[
g_{\rm BC}\le0,
\qquad
g_{\rm burden}\le0.
\]

Use an inequality augmented Lagrangian:

\[
\mathcal L_{\rm AL}
=\mathcal L_{\rm primary}
+\sum_k\lambda_k[g_k]_+
+\frac{\rho_k}{2}[g_k]_+^2,
\]

\[
\lambda_k\leftarrow[\lambda_k+\rho_k g_k]_+.
\]

Multiplier updates, penalty updates and constraint violations must be logged. If gradient conflict
remains after robust normalization, add one separately controlled gradient method, preferably
CAGrad or PCGrad; do not combine several methods in the primary experiment.

## 8. H4–H80 stochastic observable curriculum

Sample decoded horizons from

\[
\mathcal H_{obs}=\{4,8,16,32,80\}
\]

using a declared probability `p(h)` and the unbiased objective

\[
\mathbb E_{h\sim p}
\left[
\frac{\alpha_h}{p(h)}\mathcal L_{obs}^{(h)}
\right].
\]

This exposes H80 physics during training without decoding all horizons in every batch.

## 9. Phase-1 module boundaries

Recommended modules:

- `jka_model/observables/scaling.py`: train-only robust scales and checkpoint state;
- `jka_model/optimization/augmented_lagrangian.py`: generic inequality constraints;

- `jka_model/adaptive/error_attribution.py`: data/reconstruction/nominal/adaptive decomposition;
- problem adapters: observable values and constraint definitions only;
- trainer: orchestration, never cylinder mathematics.

## 10. Phase-1 acceptance

Phase 1 passes only if:

1. attribution and gradient audit artifacts are complete;
2. normalization is finite and train-only for all seeds;
3. all 18 runs remain finite and long-rollout stable;
4. divergence and boundary non-inferiority satisfy the nested seed gate;
5. H80 observable evidence is present during training and locked evaluation;
6. no latent/rollout material regression exceeds the declared tolerance;
7. force loss no longer exhibits seed-scale singularity.

`REPRESENTATION_BLOCKED` and proceeds to Phase 3 only after Phase 2's identifiability audit; it must
not hide the result by increasing penalties indefinitely.

# Phase 2 — Identifiable Static/Dynamic Adaptive Koopman

## 11. Phase-2 scientific question

\[
\boxed{
\text{After condition effects are isolated, does history contain a distinct operator innovation?}
}
\]

## 12. Factorized generator

Replace the confounded context-to-operator map by

\[
\boxed{
A_t
=A_0
+\underbrace{\sum_{j=1}^{r_s}\phi_j(\hat q_t)B_j}_{\text{condition branch}}
+\underbrace{\sum_{k=1}^{r_d}\xi_{t,k}C_k}_{\text{dynamic innovation branch}}
}
\]

with

\[
\hat q_t=Q(z_{t-H:t}),
\qquad
\xi_t=H(z_{t-H:t},\hat q_t).
\]

The known-condition route supplies true `q_t`; the latent-inferred route supplies `Q(history)`.
Both use the same `B_j`, `C_k`, propagation, budget and gates.

Use identifiability constraints:

\[
\mathbb E[\xi_t\mid\hat q_t]=0,
\qquad
\langle B_j,C_k\rangle_F=0,
\]

and orthonormal/normalized operator bases. Do not impose a variance floor on `xi`; a zero dynamic
innovation is a valid scientific result when data do not support memory dependence.

## 13. Condition observer

Before operator training, measure whether history identifies the condition:

\[
\hat q_t=Q(z_{t-H:t}).
\]

Report held-out errors for Reynolds number, inlet velocity and condition rate. The known route may
supervise `Q` during training, but latent-inferred evaluation must not access condition labels.

If condition observability fails, mark `LATENT_CONDITION_NOT_IDENTIFIABLE`; do not attribute the
failure to low-rank Koopman capacity.

## 14. Identifiability benchmark extension

Keep the same cylinder-wake PDE and geometry, but add versioned V0.9-added problem variants:

- `80 -> 120` and `120 -> 80`;
- multiple ramp rates;
- different post-transition dwell times;
- repeated/cyclic `80 -> 120 -> 80` schedules;
- matched samples with nearly equal current `Re` and latent state but different histories;
- matched current conditions with different `dRe/dt`.

The central paired test is

\[
q_t^{(a)}\approx q_t^{(b)},
\quad z_t^{(a)}\approx z_t^{(b)},
\quad h_t^{(a)}\ne h_t^{(b)}.
\]

History dependence is supported only if the future distributions differ and the dynamic branch
outperforms the matched static branch on held-out paired trajectories.

## 15. Required controls

For identical splits, seeds, ranks and budgets, evaluate:

1. nominal `A0`;
2. global/static mean correction;
3. condition-only branch `A(q_t)`;
4. condition plus dynamic innovation;
5. shuffled-history dynamic innovation;
6. known-condition and latent-inferred condition observers.

For abrupt discrete switching, a mixture-of-Koopman model may be a secondary alternative. It must
not replace the continuous low-rank primary route without a separate result ID.

## 16. Phase-2 acceptance

Phase 2 passes only if:

1. known-condition adaptation passes its nested gates;
2. the condition observer passes its predeclared held-out accuracy gate;
3. the dynamic branch beats the capacity-matched condition-only branch by the declared material
   margin on history-identifiable pairs;
4. real history beats shuffled history;
5. `xi` is condition-centered and operator bases satisfy orthogonality tolerances;
6. Phase-1 physical non-inferiority and H80 stability remain passing;
7. results are consistent on at least 2/3 backbone/data seeds for scientific support.

If condition-only passes but dynamic innovation does not, report
`PARAMETERIZED_KOOPMAN_SUPPORTED; HISTORY_ADAPTATION_NOT_REQUIRED`. This is a valid result, not a
workflow failure.

# Phase 3 — Physical-Manifold Representation and Matched Route Study

## 17. Phase-3 scientific question

\[
\boxed{
\text{Is the remaining physical error caused by the frozen representation, and can a
physics-consistent representation remove it without destroying Koopman structure?}
}
\]

Phase 3 is entered after the Phase-1 attribution audit and Phase-2 identifiability result. It is the
first phase allowed to modify representation parameters.

## 18. Physics-consistent decoder candidates

### 18.1 Differentiable Helmholtz–Hodge projection

\[
\tilde u=D_u(z),
\qquad
u=\Pi_{\rm div}\tilde u
=\tilde u-\nabla\phi,
\]

\[
\nabla^2\phi=\nabla\cdot\tilde u.
\]

The projection must respect the cylinder, inlet, outlet and wall boundary conditions; a periodic
spectral projection alone is not sufficient for this domain.

### 18.2 Stream-function decoder

For two-dimensional incompressible velocity,

\[
u=\partial_y\psi,
\qquad
v=-\partial_x\psi,
\]

which gives divergence-free velocity by construction. Boundary lifting must enforce prescribed
inlet and no-slip cylinder/wall conditions; pressure remains a separate decoded variable.

### 18.3 Latent/physical consistency

Any projected decoder must include

\[
E(\Pi_{\rm phys}(D(z)))\approx z
\]

and reconstruction/JEPA predictive consistency. A post-hoc projection that is invisible to the
latent model is not acceptable as the primary route.

## 19. Matched comparison routes

Run three explicitly named routes:

### `frozen`

- V0.8 encoder, decoder, JEPA representation, context and `A0` frozen;
- train only the Phase-2 adaptive operator;
- remains the primary mechanism-identification result.

### `joint`

- initialize from the same V0.8 handoff;
- train the physical decoder/projection, a tightly scoped encoder tail or latent alignment layer,
  condition observer and adaptive operator;
- use lower learning rates and preserve `A0`/representation drift diagnostics.

### `from_scratch`

- initialize representation, context and operator from scratch on the same variable-condition
  data;
- use it only as a capacity/control comparison, not as a continuation of validated V0.8 evidence.

All routes must match:

- train/validation/test trajectories;
- backbone/data seeds and operator initializations;
- parameter or compute budget, with both reported;
- epochs and early-stopping contract;
- observable scaling and augmented-Lagrangian gates;
- locked-test metrics and controls.

## 20. Phase-3 acceptance

Phase 3 succeeds when:

1. the physical decoder satisfies divergence and boundary constraints at reconstruction level;
2. H80 decoded observables satisfy absolute and nominal-relative gates;
3. long latent and physical rollouts remain finite and stable;
4. the dynamic/condition conclusions from Phase 2 remain valid;
5. joint improvement exceeds frozen under matched gates and is not explained only by parameter
   count;
6. all evidence artifacts are complete for 3 seeds x 3 initializations x both condition modes;
7. V1.0 readiness is declared only if the inherited strict V0.8 handoff and all 3/3 V0.9 backbone
   seeds pass.

If `from_scratch` wins while `joint` and `frozen` fail, the conclusion is representation-route
incompatibility, not successful continuation of the current Koopman chain.

## 21. Three-phase decision tree

```text
Phase 1: Are objective scales and constraints correct?
  |-- no  -> repair scaling/ALM/horizon exposure and repeat Phase 1 only
  `-- yes -> Is the frozen representation the physical error floor?
              |-- yes -> record REPRESENTATION_BLOCKED; continue diagnostics, then Phase 3
              `-- no  -> proceed to Phase 2

Phase 2: Does history add identifiable operator information beyond condition?
  |-- no, condition works -> PARAMETERIZED_KOOPMAN_SUPPORTED
  |-- no, condition fails -> DATA_OR_REPRESENTATION_NOT_IDENTIFIABLE
  `-- yes -> DYNAMIC_ADAPTIVE_KOOPMAN_SUPPORTED; proceed to Phase 3 comparison

Phase 3: Does a physical-manifold representation improve matched evidence?
  |-- frozen wins       -> retain frozen route
  |-- joint wins        -> adopt scoped joint route
  |-- from_scratch wins -> report chain incompatibility; do not claim inherited validation
  `-- none pass         -> reassess remaining residual before any closure
```

## 23. Techniques adopted for study

The route should evaluate, not blindly combine, the following established ideas:

- augmented-Lagrangian equality/inequality constraints for physics-informed optimization;
- train-only robust observable scaling;
- PCGrad or CAGrad after measured gradient conflict;
- input-affine/bilinear Koopman generators for controlled dynamics;
- projected constrained dynamics and differentiable Helmholtz–Hodge layers;
- stream-function/curl parameterizations for hard incompressibility;
- matched frozen, joint and from-scratch ablations.

## 24. Final target after three phases

The three phases are intended to produce one of several scientifically useful conclusions, not to
force a predetermined PASS:

1. a physically non-inferior frozen adaptive Koopman model;
2. an identifiable parameterized Koopman model with no necessary history innovation;
3. an identifiable dynamic adaptive Koopman model;
4. evidence that joint physical representation learning is required;
5. or a bounded negative result showing that the remaining residual requires later closure or a
   different problem family.

Only outcomes 1–3 with strict inherited readiness and 3/3 final seed support may advance to V1.0.
All other outcomes remain valuable route decisions with explicit mathematical reasons.
