# Novelty positioning

V0.7 does not claim invention of Mori–Zwanzig closure, delay models, Koopman autoencoders, or neural residual correction.

Its project-specific contribution is an auditable experiment built on a validated physics-constrained JEPA–Koopman backbone: frozen online residual targets, exact variable-dt propagation, matched instantaneous/history/shuffled controls, closed-loop physical diagnostics, standalone provenance, and explicit negative-result handling.

Any manuscript statement must be phrased as evidence for or against finite-history predictability of this model’s residual on the tested problem. Without external CFD/experimental calibration it remains a reduced-model diagnostic, not a quantitative physical predictor.
